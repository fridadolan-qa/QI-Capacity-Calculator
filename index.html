The three-layer model

As AutoQA matures, the QA team's role shifts from primary auditor to validator of AI. Each layer is a distinct defense against quality failure, with humans operating at the highest-signal point — auditing the auditor, not raw tickets.

Layer 1 — First defense
AI Analysis (AutoQA)
  • Scores 100% of ticket volume against QA rubric
  • No sampling — full population coverage per queue
  • Identifies DSAT drivers, behavior patterns, policy gaps
  • Produces a scored dataset for all downstream layers
  • Human involvement: none at this layer
Layer 2 — Second defense
AI Audit Bot (spot-check)
  • Independent AI re-grades a statistically significant sample of Layer 1 scores
  • Sample sized using Cochran formula per queue (confidence + margin of error)
  • Surfaces model drift, calibration gaps, prompt failures, edge-case errors
  • Produces an accuracy rate and disagreement log for QA review
  • Human involvement: none — fully automated
Layer 3 — Human in the loop
QA Team (HITL)
  • Reviews Layer 2 disagreements and high-signal flagged cases
  • Maintains statistical significance at the human-review level per queue
  • Owns calibration decisions, rubric updates, and override authority
  • Outputs coaching signals and model recalibration triggers
  • Human involvement: focused, high-judgment, lower volume
